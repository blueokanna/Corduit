Derive CORDUIT_API_VERSION from the crate version; fix Cargo.toml category

- src/rpc/mod.rs: CORDUIT_API_VERSION was a hand-maintained "0.2.0" that had
  drifted from the manifest (0.1.3) and was surfaced by corduit_api_version().
  Derive it from env!("CARGO_PKG_VERSION") so the wire contract and the
  manifest share a single source of truth and can never drift again.
- Cargo.toml: replace the "asynchronous" category with "no-std" - the engine
  is synchronous by design and no_std-ready, so the old category was wrong.

Verified: clippy/fmt clean, 1.78 check clean, tests 481 passed (including
ffi/rpc api_version_is_semver_like).
